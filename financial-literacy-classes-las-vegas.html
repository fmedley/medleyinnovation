Local Classes

In-Person Financial Literacy Classes Across Southern Nevada

Our financial literacy classes in Las Vegas cover the money skills people use every day: budgeting, banking, building and repairing credit, avoiding scams, and preparing to buy a home.

Most classes are delivered with a local partner, such as a nonprofit, a workforce center, a school, or an employer, at that partner's location. Organizations can bring a full workshop series to their participants, and employers can book financial wellness workshops for their teams.

Individuals and families are welcome to contact us about enrollment and we will let you know what is currently available.

Class Topics

Financial Literacy Class Topics

Classes are drawn from our seven programs and matched to your group.

📒

Budgeting & Saving

Household budgeting, managing monthly expenses, and building emergency savings.

💳

Credit Building & Repair

Understanding credit scores, disputing errors, and rebuilding damaged credit.

🏦

Banking Basics

Opening and managing accounts, understanding fees, and using online and mobile banking safely.

🔑

Homeownership Readiness

Credit preparation, down payment planning, and understanding the home buying process.

🛡️

Fraud & Scam Prevention

Recognizing and avoiding common financial scams, including scams that target seniors.

🎓

Youth & Teen Money Skills

Teen bootcamps, student banking, and first-job readiness for ages 14–24.

🚀

Small Business Finances

Business credit, cash flow management, and access to capital for entrepreneurs.

💼

Workforce Financial Readiness

Payroll and benefits literacy, tax basics, and retirement readiness for job seekers and employees.

Common Questions

Financial Literacy Classes in Las Vegas: FAQs

Where are your Las Vegas financial literacy classes held?

We deliver on-site in the Las Vegas metro area at community organizations, workforce centers, and employer sites. Classes are also available live via Zoom.

Can individuals sign up for a class?

Yes, individuals and families can contact us about enrollment and we will let you know what is currently available. Most of our classes are delivered through partner organizations.

How much do the classes cost?

It depends on the program and how it is funded. Our programs are structured to qualify for grant funding through partner agencies. Contact us and we will explain the options for your situation.

What topics do the classes cover?

Budgeting, banking, credit building and repair, homeownership readiness, fraud prevention, youth money skills, small business finances, and workforce financial readiness.

Do you offer classes in Spanish?

Bilingual capability is available for Spanish-speaking populations.

Do you serve areas outside Las Vegas?

Yes. We deliver in person throughout Southern Nevada, and virtually via Zoom to organizations in all 50 states.

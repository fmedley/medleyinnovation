About the Workshops

What an Employee Financial Wellness Program Covers

An employee financial wellness program gives your team practical money skills they can use right away: reading a pay stub, building a budget, managing credit and debt, and getting started with retirement savings.

Our workshops draw on the same curriculum as our Workforce Financial Readiness program, and can add modules from Digital & Banking Access or Community Financial Wellness, such as fraud prevention and homeownership readiness.

Sessions are live and interactive, led by trained facilitators, with bilingual capability available for Spanish-speaking teams.

Common Questions

Employee Financial Wellness Workshops: FAQs

What is an employee financial wellness workshop?

It is a live training session that teaches employees practical money skills, such as budgeting, managing credit and debt, understanding pay and benefits, and saving for emergencies and retirement.

Do you deliver workshops at our workplace?

Yes. We deliver on-site at employer sites in the Las Vegas metro area, and live via Zoom for employers anywhere in the United States.

How long are the workshops?

We offer half-day, full-day, and multi-week formats, customized to your goals and your team's schedule.

Can the content be customized for our employees?

Yes. We customize financial literacy and workforce readiness training to your workforce, and you choose the modules.

Are workshops available in Spanish?

Bilingual capability is available for Spanish-speaking populations.

How do we get pricing?

Pricing depends on the format and the number of participants. Request a proposal and we typically respond within 3 business days.

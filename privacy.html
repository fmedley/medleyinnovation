Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Medley Innovation (“Medley Innovation,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you visit medleyinnovation.com (the “Site”) or contact us through it. Medley Innovation is based in Las Vegas, Nevada.

Information You Provide to Us

When you complete a form on our Site, such as our contact form or our proposal request form, we collect the information you choose to enter. Depending on the form, this may include your name, email address, phone number, organization, the program or funding source you are interested in, and the contents of your message.

We only collect this information when you submit it to us. You are not required to provide it to browse the Site.

Information Collected Automatically

Like most websites, our hosting and service providers may automatically log basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, device information, and the pages you view. This information is used to operate and secure the Site and is not used to identify you personally.

Our Site is served over a secure HTTPS connection.

Cookies and Tracking

Our Site does not use advertising cookies, and we do not use analytics tools such as Google Analytics or tracking pixels such as the Meta/Facebook pixel. We do not track you across other websites.

We use Google Search Console to monitor how our Site performs in Google Search. Search Console reports aggregated search data to us and does not place tracking cookies on your device through our Site.

How We Use the Information You Provide

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide the information, programs, or proposals you request;
  • To communicate with you about a partnership, program, or service you have asked about;
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Site;
  • To comply with applicable law.

Email Communications and Your Choices

Today, we use your email address to respond to messages you send us. In the future, we may send occasional updates, newsletters, or program information to people who have contacted us or opted in.

If we begin sending those messages, every one will include a way to unsubscribe, and we will honor opt-out requests promptly in keeping with the CAN-SPAM Act. You can also opt out at any time by emailing support@medleyinnovation.com. We will continue to send necessary replies to inquiries you initiate.

How We Share Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only in these limited situations:

  • With service providers that help us operate the Site and our forms (described below), acting on our behalf;
  • When required by law, or to protect our rights, safety, or property.

Third-Party Services

We rely on a few third-party services to run the Site. Each may process limited technical information, such as your IP address, to deliver its part of the Site:

  • Form delivery: our forms are processed by FormSubmit, which forwards your submission to our email;
  • Website hosting: the Site is hosted on GitHub Pages;
  • Fonts: fonts are served by Google Fonts.

These providers have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

External Links

Our Site links to third-party websites, including government and nonprofit resources, for your convenience. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those sites, and this policy does not apply to them.

How We Protect Your Information

We use reasonable measures to protect the information you submit, including serving the Site over HTTPS. However, no method of transmitting or storing information over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices and Access

You may ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing support@medleyinnovation.com. We will respond as required by applicable law.

Children’s Privacy

Our Site is directed to organizations and adults, not to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through this Site. Youth financial literacy programs are arranged through partner organizations, schools, and agencies, not through data collected from children on this Site.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date above. Your continued use of the Site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy or your dealings with this website, contact us at support@medleyinnovation.com.
